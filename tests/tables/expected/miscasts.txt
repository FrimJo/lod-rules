1|Through luck or skill, the wizard manages to shape the spell and change the outcome at the very last second. Any DMG or Healing effect of the spell is maximized. Any other spell is cast without the need to spend any Mana.
2|The wizard consumes Mana as if the spell succeeded.
3|The Wizard loses control of the spell and it takes a tremendous effort to stop if from turning into a complete disaster. The Wizard loses 3 times the Mana cost for the spell.
4|The wizard’s mind is wracked by images of the void. The Wizard loses 1d3 point of Sanity.
5|The Wizard becomes unconscious and falls to the floor. The wizard must make a CON test at the start of his activation to see if he wakes up.
6|Confused, the Wizard realises he has forgotten the spell halfway through the casting. Mana is consumed just as a failed spell, and the Wizard is not allowed to use the spell again until he has rested in a tavern.
7|A searing pain shoots through the mind of the Wizard. He loses 1d6 HP with no armour or Natural Armour.
8|Visions of the Void is portrayed in the minds of all friendly characters within 3 squares. Not used to seeing this, all characters lose 1d8 points of Sanity and the Wizard loses 1d3 points of Sanity.
9|The wizard manages to open a portal to the Void and Demons pour through. Roll on the Demon table to see what materialises.
10|A magic blast in unleashed from the Wizard, causing 1D10 DMG to any character within 3 squares, ignoring armour and Natural armour.
